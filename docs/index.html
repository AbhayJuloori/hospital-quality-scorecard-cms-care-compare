Hospital Quality Scorecard

Five public CMS Care Compare files (star ratings, HRRP readmissions, HCAHPS patient survey, healthcare-associated infections, ED timeliness) joined across three annual releases in DuckDB SQL. Every suppressed, footnoted or unavailable cell is flagged, every KPI is tied back to the published CMS values, and Indiana and New Jersey hospitals are compared with state and national acute care peers. Code and method on GitHub.
